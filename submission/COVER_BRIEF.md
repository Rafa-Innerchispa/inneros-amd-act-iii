# Cover Brief

## Text
**INNEROS**

**Governed AI Operations on AMD**

**Reason. Act. Prove.**

Optional small line:
**Gemma reasoning · Evolus workflow · Verifiable evidence**

## Composition
Create a cinematic, sober 16:9 technology image.

Center: a luminous InnerOS control-plane core.

Left: AMD compute fabric, abstract GPU/ROCm infrastructure.

Right: three clean stages flowing outward:
1. reasoning;
2. governed action;
3. verified evidence receipt.

Bottom-right may include a subtle real-world smart-building silhouette to imply Bellini without turning the cover into a building-management product.

## Visual rules
- premium, restrained, technically credible;
- dark background;
- cyan/electric-blue compute paths;
- minimal warm accent for governance/approval;
- no fake dashboards;
- no tiny unreadable text;
- no robot heads;
- no generic “AI brain” stock-art look;
- no unsupported benchmark numbers;
- AMD is the compute hero, InnerOS is the product hero.

## Goal
A judge should understand in one glance:
**AI intelligence → controlled action → proof.**
